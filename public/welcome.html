A Co. 15th MI BN (AE)

Accountability that is done before the formation breaks.

Platoon Accountability answers one question fast: who is here, who is out, and why. Built for the NCO holding a phone at 0630, not for a desk.

Sign in What it does

Formation mode

One soldier on screen at a time, in rank order, unaccounted first. Anyone already on leave or TDY is skipped and shown at the end so a wrong one can be fixed on the spot.

Absences that close themselves

Schedule leave, a pass, TDY or a school once. It activates on the start date, returns the soldier to duty on the end date, and files itself in their history. No one has to remember to undo it.

Who is free on a given day

Ask for a date or a range and get the soldiers who are actually available, read from the schedule rather than from today's roster.

Duty roster with conflict checks

Post duty and get told immediately if the soldier you picked is already on leave, at a school, or otherwise committed that day.

Soldier records

Contact info, next of kin, MOS, date of rank, ETS, readiness dates and absence history in one place, with an audit trail on every change.

Invite only

No open sign-up. An admin issues a single-use link that carries the platoons and the level of access, and it expires on its own.

This is an internal tool for A Co. 15th MI BN (AE). It is not an official Army system of record and it does not replace eMILPO, IPPS-A, DTMS, or any other system of record.
