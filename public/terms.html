1. About Platoon Accountability

Platoon Accountability is a software tool used by a unit to track daily personnel accountability for its formations. It provides tools for recording duty status, scheduling and tracking absences such as leave, passes, TDY and schools, maintaining a personnel directory, posting a duty roster, checking availability on a given date, and producing accountability reports.

Platoon Accountability is a convenience tool only. It is not an official system of record of the United States Army or the Department of Defense, it is not endorsed by them, and it does not replace eMILPO, IPPS-A, DTMS, DTS, or any other authoritative system. It does not grant leave, approve passes, issue orders, or make any determination of a soldier's status.
