Built around first formation

Everything else in the app exists to make those three minutes shorter and the report afterwards correct.

Formation mode

One soldier on screen at a time, in rank order, unaccounted first. Anyone already on leave or TDY is skipped and handed back at the end, so a wrong one gets fixed on the spot.

Absences that close themselves

Schedule leave, a pass, TDY or a school once. It activates on the start date, returns the soldier to duty on the end date, and files itself in their history. Nobody has to remember to undo it.

The report, already written

Assigned, accounted, needs action, leave and pass, TDY and school, all counted as you go. Copy it into the message you were going to send anyway, or export the roster as CSV.

Directory

The whole roster, grouped the way you brief it

Every soldier under your unit, by platoon, with current status and return date in the same row. Search by name, role or position; filter by unit, status or who is coming back this week.

  • Nested units: company, platoon, squad, section, team
  • You see your own units and the ones under them, nothing above or beside
  • Sortable columns on every table

Soldier records

One record per soldier, and an audit trail behind it

Contact details, emergency contact and next of kin, administrative data and readiness dates in one place, with every absence they have taken listed underneath.

  • Medical, dental, weapons qualification and ETS dates
  • Absence history, kept rather than deleted on return

Your roster is your unit's

One organization cannot see another

Before you sign up

Is this an official Army system?

No. Platoon Manager is a unit-run accountability tool. It is not an official Army or Department of Defense system of record, it is not endorsed by them, and it does not replace eMILPO, IPPS-A, DTMS or anything else of that kind. Do not enter classified information into it.

Do we have to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer, and it can be added to a phone's home screen so it opens like an app. There is nothing to push through a device manager.

Can another unit see our roster?

No. Each organization's data is separated at the database level, and inside your own organization a leader only sees their own units and the ones below them. The privacy policy has the detail.

What happens when the trial ends?

You are told before it does, and you can extend it once from inside the app. After that the account is locked rather than deleted. Subscribe and everything is where you left it.

How do we get our data out?

An owner can export the whole organization as one file at any time: units, personnel, profiles, absences, duty roster, saved reports and settings. The same file restores it.
